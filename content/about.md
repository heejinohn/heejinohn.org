I am an assistant professor of accounting at the [Price College of Business](https://www.ou.edu/price), University of Oklahoma. Beginning in January 2027, I will join the Accounting & Information Systems Department at Villanova University as a visiting assistant professor.

I earned my PhD in Accounting from the University of Iowa, after completing a Master of Accountancy at Iowa and a Bachelor of Business Administration at Korea University. Before entering academia, I worked as an audit associate with KPMG Korea and served as a financial management officer in the Republic of Korea Air Force.

My research examines financial reporting, capital markets and investor information acquisition, and my work has been published in [*The Accounting Review*](/research/measuring-cash-flows/).

My [research](/research/) is listed on this site, and my [CV](/cv.pdf) has more detail.
